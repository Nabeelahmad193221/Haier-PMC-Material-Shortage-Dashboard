## 🔷 Project Overview

The **Haier PMC Material Shortage Dashboard** is a Power BI analytics project designed to improve visibility into material requirements, available inventory, and production-related shortage risks.

It brings key material planning information into a centralized reporting interface to support inventory monitoring, shortage identification, and production planning decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor required material quantities and available stock.
- Identify material shortages and critical inventory risks.
- Analyze shortages by product model, priority, shift, and department.
- Track material requirements and shortage trends.
- Support replenishment planning and production continuity.
- Enable data-driven operational decisions.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Business Purpose |
|:---|:---|
| **Total Required Quantity** | Measures total material demand. |
| **Available Stock** | Shows the quantity of material available. |
| **Shortage Quantity** | Highlights gaps between demand and available stock. |
| **Overall Availability %** | Measures stock coverage against required quantities. |
| **Critical Materials** | Identifies materials requiring urgent attention. |

> KPI definitions and values should be verified against the actual dataset and DAX measures.
